Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how Home Backup (“the app”) accesses, uses and stores data, including data received from Google APIs. The app is a personal backup tool that runs on the user's own computer. The developer does not operate any server for it and does not receive any of your data.

1. Data the app accesses

Google user data. When you sign in with Google, the app requests only the following permission (OAuth scope):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — access to the files and folders in your Google Drive that the app itself has created.

The app cannot see, read, modify or delete any other files in your Google Drive. It does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, profile information or any other Google service.

Local files. The app reads the folders you choose to back up on your own computer.

2. How the data is used

Access to Google Drive is used only to provide the backup function you run yourself:

  • create a backup folder and upload backup archives to it;
  • list and download those archives when you check or restore a backup;
  • delete old archives according to the retention settings you configure.

Google user data is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or for developing, improving or training artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

3. Sharing and disclosure

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data or any other data to the developer or to any third party. The app communicates only with Google's servers, directly from your computer.

Home Backup's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage

  • The OAuth token that lets the app access your Google Drive is stored only on your computer, in a file readable only by your user account.
  • All communication with Google uses HTTPS.
  • The developer has no copy of your files or tokens.

5. Retention and deletion

Backups stay in your Google Drive until the app removes them under your retention settings or you delete them yourself. You can delete the backup folder in Google Drive at any time. To stop the app from accessing your Google Drive, revoke its access on your Google Account's Third-party apps & services page and delete the token file from your computer.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
